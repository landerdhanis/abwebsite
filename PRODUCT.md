# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Antwerps Badhuis: a private wellness venue in Antwerp that a group rents exclusively for a time slot. Facilities: sauna, hammam, jacuzzi and a heated 32°C indoor pool. Also hosts children's parties, bachelor parties (vrijgezellen) and family events, and offers swimming lessons for children and for women.

## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro (static output). Chosen by the user, 2026-09-25.

## Users
- Primary: couples and friend groups aged 25–45 booking a private wellness slot. (User confirmed the example in the brief.)
- Secondary: parents planning a children's party; parents looking for swimming lessons; women looking for women-only swimming lessons.
- Most of them visit on a phone.

## Product Purpose
Get the visitor to book a time slot. The "Reserveer" action is visible on every page and one tap away on mobile. Booking happens on an external booking URL (NOT YET PROVIDED).

## Positioning
Premium consumer / boutique hotel spa: calm, warm and a bit luxurious. It must never read as a municipal pool. The key difference: the whole place is yours, with no strangers.

## Operating Context
Flemish (Dutch) language. Visitors compare offers and check price and opening hours before booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Offers must be easy to tell apart: Wellness (private rental), Feestjes & events, Zwemlessen.
- Prices and opening hours must be reachable in one step.
- Pages: Home, Wellness, Feestjes & events, Zwemlessen, Prijzen & info, Foto's, FAQ, Contact.
- Never invent prices, reviews, ratings, capacities or hours. Mark anything missing visibly as "ontbreekt".

## Brand Commitments
- Dials: DESIGN_VARIANCE 6, MOTION_INTENSITY 4, VISUAL_DENSITY 3. Airy, slow and quiet motion, distinctive but not chaotic.
- No perpetual looping animation. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Evidence on Hand
- Missing: real photos, prices, opening hours, address, phone, email, FAQ answers, booking URL, capacity per slot.
- 32°C pool temperature and the four facilities are confirmed in the brief.

## Product Principles
1. Privacy is the product: say "only your group" before anything else.
2. The price and the next step are never more than one tap away.
3. Calm beats clever.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-reachable booking, reduced-motion support, large tap targets for phone use.
